## Overview

Contributed to high-level robot strategy and architecture, led the **Funnel** and **Stick** subsystems, trained the new drive team, and helped the team succeed at competitions as Drive Coach.

## Goals

- Build two high-quality robots and qualify for the World Championship
- **Funnel** as wide as possible with robust game piece acquisition
- **Funnel** must clear space for the climber at the end of the match
- **Stick** passively manipulates the game piece

## Funnel

<figure class="figure-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/stack-up-redundancy/funnel-cad.jpg" alt="Funnel CAD"><figcaption>Funnel CAD</figcaption></figure>

- Motor-driven opposing wheels and wings
- Retro-reflective tape and sensor to detect the game piece
- Mounted to angled crossbeams for the elevator
- Wings close to intake the game piece, then open to clear space for the climber

## Stick

<figure class="figure-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/stack-up-redundancy/stick-cad.jpg" alt="Stick CAD"><figcaption>Stick CAD</figcaption></figure>

- Added to the completed robot without an additional motor
- Extreme weight reduction to stay under 1 lb
- One-way bearing enables a passive four-bar linkage
- Surgical tubing spring ensures two discrete positions
- Custom rubber wheel for game piece manipulation

## Results

- Competed at 7 events: **3x District Event Winner**, **New England Championship Division Winner**
- **Ranked 1st in New England** in district points
- Received the Quality, Industrial Design, and Creativity Awards
- One of the best intake systems at the 2025 FRC World Championship
