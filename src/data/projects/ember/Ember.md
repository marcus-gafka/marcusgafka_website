## Overview

Led the initial scoping, rapid design, and integrated assembly of Ember, an experimental swerve-drive chassis with a ground cube intake.

## Goals

- Create an experimental swerve-drive robot for 190 to practice programming and driving
- Onboard a small group of college mentors
- Teach incoming students prototyping and fabrication
- Go from project start to functional robot in only **3 weeks**
