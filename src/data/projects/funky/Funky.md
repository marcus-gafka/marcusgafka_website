## Overview

Brainstormed, designed, manufactured, assembled, and iterated on Funky side by side with students: a test platform for prototyping software and mechanical systems.

## Goals

- Prepare Team 190 for the 2025 build season
- Enable early-season driver practice
- Focus on modularity to adapt the robot as needed

**Chassis**

- Box tube and gusset construction with cross-members for robustness
- Mounting rails on either side for easy integration
- Designed to quickly add other systems

**Added systems**

- Electrical components fully integrated and operational
- Ballast to simulate the dynamics of the competition robot
- Upper body of the 2025 Kitbot for early practice
- Vision system so the software team could work on camera placement and field localization

## Results

- Enabled early development and testing of both mechanical and software systems
- Gave the drive team weeks of extra driver practice
- Enabled experimentation with AprilTag detection, vision-based localization, and autonomous routines
- Provided a reliable, adaptable platform for subsystem prototyping, contributing directly to the 2025 robot's success
- Doubled as a defensive robot for practicing against aggressive opponents
