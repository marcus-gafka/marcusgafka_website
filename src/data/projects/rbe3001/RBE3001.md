## Overview

<figure class="figure-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/rbe3001/arm.jpg" alt="OpenManipulator-X arm picking up a green ball"><figcaption>Picking up a green ball</figcaption></figure>

In a team of four, programmed a 4-DOF robot arm in MATLAB to move smoothly and use camera data to detect objects and sort them into compartments by color, implementing forward and inverse kinematics with DH transformations.
