## Goals

- Build a high-quality, competitive robot
- **Intake** must absorb impacts without breaking
- **Intake** must be full width with robust acquisition

## Intake

<figure class="figure-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/snapback/intake-cad.jpg" alt="Intake CAD"><figcaption>Intake CAD</figcaption></figure>

- Pneumatic-piston-powered four-bar linkage
- Rollers driven by belts from a motor mounted low in the chassis
- Makes full use of the allowed 18-inch extension
- Stows compactly above the Centralizer

## Centralizer

<figure class="figure-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/snapback/centralizer-cad.jpg" alt="Centralizer CAD"><figcaption>Centralizer CAD</figcaption></figure>

- Opposing wheels powered by a single motor
- Moves the game piece to the center of the robot
- Magnetic door cover for battery access

## Results

- Competed at 11 events: **2x District Event Finalist**, **World Championship Curie Division Winner**
- Ranked 32nd in New England in district points
- Quality and Excellence in Engineering Awards at district events
- Intake and Centralizer met their goals and consistently outperformed expectations
