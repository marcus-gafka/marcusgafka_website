## Overview

In the 2026 season I held many roles on the team at the same time:

- **Prototyping Lead:** managed concurrent prototypes of potential robot subsystems
- **Hopper Lead:** as mentor roles shifted over the season, led the design of the V2 robot's complex hopper subsystem
- **Drive Coach:** continued running the team on the field at competitions

## Prototyping

- Managed concurrent prototypes of various potential robot subsystems
- Helped with the intake, many prototype hopper mechanisms, and full robot layout concepts
