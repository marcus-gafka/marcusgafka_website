## Overview

In a team of three first-year students, built a VEX robot to compete in a class-wide competition, backing up the design with engineering calculations.

## Goals

- Design, build, and program (in Python) a robot competitive in the RBE1001 game
- Use sensors and state machines for reliable autonomous routines
- Document decisions, calculations, and progress in a final report

## Robot

<figure class="figure-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/rbe1001/robot.jpg" alt="Completed RBE1001 robot"><figcaption>Our completed robot</figcaption></figure>

- Conveyor belt that carries 4 balls at a time
- Grabber with a rubber tip for tipping the bucket
- Fast four-motor drivetrain for speed and torque
- High-traction wheels for climbing the steep ramp
- Ultrasonic, line, camera, and gyroscope sensors
