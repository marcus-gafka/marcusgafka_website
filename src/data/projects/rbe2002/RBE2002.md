## Overview

In a team of three, programmed a robot to autonomously complete a series of challenges: PID control, state machines, line following, AprilTag detection and tracking, strain gauge measurement, hysteresis, gyroscope optimization, and more.

## Goals

- Program a Pololu Romi to autonomously complete the trash collection and removal demo
- Implement software controllers and filters in C++ to maximize reliability
- Understand the nuances of computer vision, sensing, and motor control

## Line Following

<figure class="figure-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/rbe2002/line-following.jpg" alt="Romi following a line"><figcaption>Following a white tape line</figcaption></figure>

- PID controller for wheel velocities
- Line sensor array to detect surface color
- Tuned PID controller for line following
- Custom logic for intersection detection
- Filtered gyroscope bias and drift for precise turns

## Final Robot

<figure class="figure-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/rbe2002/final-robot.jpg" alt="Romi with all systems"><figcaption>All final systems attached</figcaption></figure>

- Differential-drive chassis with motors and encoders
- Romi board with IR receiver, gyroscope, and more
- Array of six optical sensors mounted close to the ground
- Servo-actuated over-center lifting mechanism
- OpenMV camera mounted below the lifter

## Results

- Worked on the first attempt: collected 3 buckets and delivered them to the drop-off zone
- Finished with time to spare for extra credit challenges, earning an **A+**
- Implemented a state machine with sub-states
