Growing up so close to NASA, I had the incredible opportunity in high school to apply to and join FRC Team 118, the Robonauts, a world-renowned team that has been competing for almost 30 years. On this team I grew my mechanical, robotics, and problem-solving skills, but most importantly, I realized that engineering was my calling.

I went from a quiet, shy, unconfident kid to a true leader. I opened CAD software for the first time on this team, and by the time I graduated I was designing entire subsystems with multiple degrees of freedom and complex geometry. In my senior year I was chosen as Team Captain and for the drive team, and the mentor I worked most closely with wrote my college recommendation letters.

FRC and Team 118 were instrumental in my development as an engineer. I'm grateful to everyone involved, and I hope to pay it forward to the students I mentor now.
