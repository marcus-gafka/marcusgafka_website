## Overview

In a team of four, synthesized a mechanism that solves a real-world problem, built a working prototype, and gave a Shark Tank–style pitch to the professor.

## Goals

- Create a unique mechanism with more than 4 links and 4 joints
- Develop the CAD and build a fully functional prototype for under $30

## Final Design: Oven Door Opener

- Foot-pedal-actuated oven door opener
- Six-bar linkage in a Watt arrangement
- Laser-cut wood frame, acrylic links, and cardstock stencils
- 3D-printed PLA pedal and connectors
- Iterated on screw joints for minimal slop and friction

## Results

- Prototype was fully functional and demonstrated live
- Well received by the professor and classmates
- Received an **A+** after the product pitch and report
