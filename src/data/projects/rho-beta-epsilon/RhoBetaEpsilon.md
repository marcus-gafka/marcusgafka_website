## Overview

<figure class="figure-inline logo-inline"><img src="/assets/projects/rho-beta-epsilon/rbe-logo.png" alt="Rho Beta Epsilon logo: a gear around 0111 and an op-amp symbol"></figure>

Rho Beta Epsilon is WPI's Robotics Engineering honor society (Alpha Chapter). Its mission is *"to encourage and recognize exemplary robotics students while aiding in their development both personally and professionally through exceptional education, leadership, and passion for robotics; and to enable the advancement of the robotics engineering community."* Members support the RBE department through workshops, lab help hours, and events each term.

## Membership

Membership is selective and open to RBE majors and minors in good academic standing. Undergraduates need three full semesters at WPI and two RBE courses at the 2000 level or above. Each application cycle, at the start of A-Term and C-Term, applicants:

- Solve challenging technical questions
- Submit a résumé and statement of purpose
- Present their accomplishments in a professional interview

## My Role: New Member Officer

In September 2025 I was elected New Member Officer, responsible for bringing in each new class of members:

- Lead recruitment and induction for prospective members
- Develop the technical questions, scoring rubrics, and interview process
- Coordinate applicant interviews and new member evaluations
