Robotics is what brought me to WPI. As a high school senior in 2023, I applied for WPI's robotics scholarships for students from both the FIRST Robotics Competition and the VEX Robotics Competition, submitting a video for each. I was selected as the **2023 winner of WPI's full-tuition, merit-based FRC scholarship**.
